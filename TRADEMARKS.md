The AccountGo source code is licensed under the [GNU AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE). The **AccountGo name and logos are not**. The AGPL grants rights to the code, not to the brand. Section 7(e) of the AGPL expressly allows trademark rights to be withheld, and they are withheld here.

This policy explains how you may and may not use the AccountGo trademarks. Its purpose is simple: anyone who sees the AccountGo name should be able to trust that it refers to software and services from the AccountGo project itself.

## The marks

"AccountGo™", "AccountGo Cloud™", and the AccountGo logos (including `src/Web/public/accountgo-logo-*.svg`) are trademarks of Marvin Perez ("AccountGo", "we"). All other trademarks belong to their respective owners.

## What you can do without asking

- **Refer to the project truthfully**, for example: "built on AccountGo", "a fork of AccountGo", "compatible with AccountGo", "AccountGo integration for X".
- **Self-host an unmodified copy for your own organization's internal use** with the original name and logos, as long as you don't offer it to third parties as a product or service.
- **Discuss, review, teach, or write about AccountGo**, including using the name in articles, tutorials, talks, and books.
- **Link to the project** using the name or the unmodified logo, as long as it doesn't suggest endorsement or partnership.
- **Rebrand your tenant** with AccountGo's built-in white-label settings when you use AccountGo Cloud or an unmodified self-hosted copy.

## What requires our written permission

- **Distributing a modified version** under the AccountGo name or logos. Forks must use a different name and remove the logos; you may still say "based on AccountGo".
- **Offering AccountGo as a hosted, managed, or commercial service to third parties** under the AccountGo name or logos, e.g. "AccountGo Hosting" or "Managed AccountGo".
- **Using the marks in, or confusingly similar to, a product, company, or domain name**, e.g. `accountgo-pro.com`, "AccountGo ERP Suite", "AcctGo".
- **Using the marks in a way that suggests we endorse, sponsor, certify, or partner with you** or your product.
- **Selling merchandise** that carries the marks.
- **Modifying the logos** or combining them with other marks.

## Forks and redistributions

You're welcome to fork and redistribute the code under the AGPL. When you distribute a modified version or offer it as a service:

1. Choose a name that isn't AccountGo or confusingly similar to it.
2. Remove or replace the AccountGo logos, favicon, and in-app branding (e.g. `src/Web/public/accountgo-logo-*.svg` and product-name strings).
3. Keep the copyright and license notices the AGPL requires (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`). Those are legal notices, not branding, and must stay.
4. You may state that your product is "based on AccountGo" in its description or documentation.

Contact [support@accountgo.dev](mailto:support@accountgo.dev) to ask for permission or to report possible misuse of the marks.
