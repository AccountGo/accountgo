# US GAAP Disclosure Patterns and Checklist Design

Use this reference to generate transaction-specific US GAAP footnote disclosure checklists (`ASC 235`, `ASC 275`, and topic-specific Codification Subtopics `XX-50`) instead of generic boilerplate.

## 1) Disclosure design principles under US GAAP

- Under the FASB Codification, disclosure requirements are housed in **Section 50 (`XX-XX-50`)** of each Topic/Subtopic, supplemented by **`ASC 235` (*Notes to Financial Statements*)** and **`ASC 275` (*Risks and Uncertainties*)**.
- Treat footnote disclosure as an integral part of the US GAAP conclusion—accurate general ledger balances without required disclosures do not constitute GAAP-compliant financial statements.
- Always distinguish **Public Business Entity (PBE)** disclosure requirements from **Non-PBE (Private Company)** disclosure accommodations (many ASC Topics, including `ASC 606`, `ASC 842`, `ASC 718`, `ASC 820`, and `ASC 740`, provide reduced footnote disclosures for Non-PBEs).

## 2) Universal US GAAP disclosure checklist skeleton

# Transaction-Specific US GAAP Disclosure Checklist: [Topic / ASC Reference]

## A. Nature of operations and transaction context (`ASC 235` / `ASC 275`)
- [ ] Describe the transaction, arrangement, or event and its business purpose
- [ ] Identify related-party relationships and terms if applicable (`ASC 850-10-50`)
- [ ] State reporting period cut-off and subsequent event evaluation date (`ASC 855-10-50`)

## B. Summary of significant accounting policies (`ASC 235-10-50`)
- [ ] State the recognition and measurement principles applied
- [ ] Disclose practical expedients and accounting policy elections adopted (including PCC elections for Non-PBEs)
- [ ] Describe how multiple components (e.g., lease vs. non-lease, multiple performance obligations) are separated and allocated

## C. Quantitative financial statement line-item disclosures
- [ ] Balance sheet carrying amounts and classification (current vs. non-current under `ASC 210`)
- [ ] Income statement line items where revenues, costs, gains, or losses are recorded (`ASC 220`)
- [ ] Cash flow statement classification (`ASC 230` operating, investing, or financing activities, plus noncash investing and financing supplemental disclosures)
- [ ] Required rollforwards, reconciliations, and maturity schedules

## D. Significant judgments and estimation uncertainty (`ASC 275-10-50`)
- [ ] Nature of estimates susceptible to material near-term change
- [ ] Fair value hierarchy levels (`Level 1`, `Level 2`, `Level 3`) and valuation techniques (`ASC 820-10-50`)
- [ ] Credit loss allowance rollforwards and methodology (`ASC 326-20-50`)

## E. Accounting changes, error corrections, or transition (`ASC 250-10-50`)
- [ ] Nature of and reason for any change in accounting principle (including preferability)
- [ ] Quantitative effect on each financial statement line item and Retained Earnings

## 3) Topic-specific disclosure patterns

### Revenue from Contracts with Customers (`ASC 606-10-50` & `ASC 340-40-50`)
- [ ] **Disaggregation of revenue:** PBEs must disaggregate revenue into categories depicting how nature, amount, timing, and uncertainty of revenue and cash flows are affected by economic factors; Non-PBEs may elect simplified disaggregation based on timing of transfer (point-in-time vs. over-time) and qualitative economic factors.
- [ ] **Contract balances:** Opening and closing balances of receivables, contract assets, and contract liabilities (deferred revenue).
- [ ] **Performance obligations:** Description of when performance obligations are typically satisfied, significant payment terms, nature of goods/services, principal vs. agent judgments, and warranty/return obligations.
- [ ] **Remaining performance obligations (RPO / Backlog):** Required for PBEs (with optional practical expedients for contracts <=1 year); optional for Non-PBEs.
- [ ] **Contract costs (`ASC 340-40`):** Closing balances of capitalized costs to obtain/fulfill contracts, amortization method, amount of amortization recognized, and whether the <=1 year practical expedient was elected.

### Leases (`ASC 842-20-50`)
- [ ] Qualitative description of leases, renewal/termination options, and variable payment terms.
- [ ] Practical expedients elected (e.g., package of transition expedients, non-separation of lease/non-lease components, short-term lease recognition exemption, Non-PBE risk-free rate election).
- [ ] Quantitative breakdown of total lease cost (finance lease ROU amortization + interest, operating lease cost, short-term lease cost, variable lease cost, sublease income).
- [ ] Supplemental cash flow information (cash paid for amounts included in lease liabilities; ROU assets obtained in exchange for new lease liabilities).
- [ ] Weighted-average remaining lease term and weighted-average discount rate (separately for operating and finance leases).
- [ ] Five-year annual undiscounted cash flow maturity schedule reconciled to lease liabilities on the balance sheet.

### Contingencies and Commitments (`ASC 450-20-50` & `ASC 460-10-50`)
- [ ] For **accrued probable loss contingencies:** Nature of the contingency and amount accrued (if disclosure is necessary for financial statements not to be misleading).
- [ ] For **reasonably possible loss contingencies** (or where exposure exceeds the accrued amount): Nature of the contingency and an estimate of the possible loss or range of loss (or an explicit statement that such an estimate cannot be made).
- [ ] Guarantees and product warranty rollforwards (`ASC 460`).

### Income Taxes (`ASC 740-10-50`)
- [ ] Current and deferred tax expense/benefit components.
- [ ] Significant components of Deferred Tax Assets (DTAs), Deferred Tax Liabilities (DTLs), and any Valuation Allowance (plus net change in the valuation allowance).
- [ ] Statutory-to-effective tax rate reconciliation (tabular numerical reconciliation for PBEs; qualitative description of significant reconciling items for Non-PBEs).
- [ ] Operating loss and tax credit carryforwards and expiration dates.
- [ ] Uncertain tax positions (`ASC 740-10-50-15`): Tabular reconciliation of unrecognized tax benefits (required for PBEs; reduced requirements for Non-PBEs), interest/penalties policy, and open tax years.

## 4) Risks, uncertainties, and significant estimates (`ASC 275`)

Every complete set of US GAAP footnotes must address the four areas of `ASC 275-10-50`:
1. **Nature of operations:** Description of major products/services, principal markets, and locations.
2. **Use of estimates in the preparation of financial statements:** Explanation that preparing financial statements in conformity with US GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions.
3. **Certain significant estimates:** Disclosure when it is *reasonably possible* that a condition, situation, or set of circumstances existing at the balance sheet date will change the estimate in the near term (within 1 year) and the effect would be material.
4. **Current vulnerability due to certain concentrations:** Concentrations in customers, suppliers, revenue sources, geographic areas, or labor where risk of severe impact in the near term is reasonably possible.

## 5) Public Business Entity (PBE) vs. Non-PBE (Private Company) disclosure accommodations

Always verify whether the reporting entity is a **PBE** or a **Non-PBE** before drafting footnotes:
- **Segment Reporting (`ASC 280`):** Mandatory for public entities; not required for nonpublic entities.
- **Earnings Per Share (`ASC 260`):** Mandatory for entities with publicly traded common stock (or potential common stock); not required for nonpublic entities.
- **Fair Value (`ASC 820`), Revenue (`ASC 606`), Stock Comp (`ASC 718`), and Income Taxes (`ASC 740`):** Include specific carve-outs that reduce tabular rollforward and disaggregation burdens for Non-PBEs.

## 6) Common disclosure failure modes

- Applying Non-PBE disclosure exemptions to an entity that meets the `ASC Master Glossary` definition of a Public Business Entity (PBE)
- Omitting the `ASC 855` date through which subsequent events were evaluated
- Failing to disclose noncash investing and financing activities under `ASC 230` (e.g., ROU assets obtained for lease liabilities, equipment acquired under vendor financing, conversion of debt to equity)
- Using GAAP titles (*Balance Sheet*, *Income Statement*) on OCBOA Cash Basis or Modified Cash Basis statements without modifying statement titles and basis-of-presentation footnotes
